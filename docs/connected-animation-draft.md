# Connected animation draft

Entry: /trail, linked from /chapters. This is an illustrative 3:12 overview, not a replacement for the existing detailed chapters or geographic map.

## Scope shipped
- One Three.js player and train, eight sequential 24-second stages; no satellite or remote assets.
- Pretoria: jacaranda avenue and simplified Union Buildings silhouette.
- Johannesburg: skyline, tower and mine-dump silhouette.
- Kimberley: open pit, water and animated hoist.
- De Aar: yard tracks, signals and night sky.
- Beaufort West: koppies and moving illustrative springbok.
- Matjiesfontein: simplified hotel, veranda and moving red bus.
- Worcester: Hex River mountain silhouettes, portal and vineyard rows.
- Cape Town: Table Mountain silhouette, drifting tablecloth and coastal town.
- Pause, seek, stop selection, automatic handoffs, replay, window view, direct stop URLs.
- Matjiesfontein and Worcester links now open their draft stages. Existing detailed chapters remain intact.

## Limits and next priorities
All positions, dimensions and timing in the draft are deliberately compressed approximations. This is not the surveyed railway, an operational simulation or a validated building reconstruction. No detailed-chapter passport stamps are awarded by watching the draft.

Next: replace Matjiesfontein and Worcester blockouts with locally sourced geometry; connect detailed-chapter completion events to the next stop; reuse the existing audio pack with one shared transport; then tune landmark scale, scenery transitions and lower-end phone performance. Do not rebuild the six detailed chapters.

Validation: scripts/check-trail-draft.mjs exercises all eight stages, pause, automatic handoff, ending, window camera, and direct stop entry. Captures in evidence/trail-draft. TypeScript and production build also checked.
